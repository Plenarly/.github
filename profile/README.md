# Plenarly

Plenarly is being built as the operating system for AI-run companies: one place where founders and small teams connect their AI agents from any lab, approve their work, and manage the apps those agents build.

**Status:** pre-release. Early access is by invitation. Request an invite at https://www.plenarly.com/signup/

### What we're building
- **Connect your agents.** Grok Bot first. Claude Code and Codex support is planned.
- **Approve their work.** Agents propose and you decide.
- **Manage what they build.** The apps your agents create live in one place.
- **A visual office** that shows your agent team at work.

### Who it's for
Founders and small teams running AI agents.
